Privacy Policy — Jaegumae Curator

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Jaegumae Curator is a private tool used only by its operator to manage three Threads profiles the operator owns (@jaegumae.zip, @first.salary.note, @ssul.bakery). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

Through the official Threads API only: our own profiles' posts, replies and insights, and public Threads posts returned by keyword search.

How we use it

To schedule our own posts, reply to comments on our posts, and find relevant public posts to leave a short, relevant comment on. We do not sell or share data, do not use it for advertising, do not send direct messages, and do not build profiles of people.

Retention

Other users' public posts and usernames are kept at most 7 days (only to avoid commenting on the same post twice), then deleted. Statistics about our own posts are kept for performance reports.

Data deletion

To have any data about you removed, email ericmun0206@gmail.com with your Threads username. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email. You can also block our profiles or remove the app's access in your Threads settings.
